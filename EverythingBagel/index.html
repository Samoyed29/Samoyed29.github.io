How to Play

Follow these quick steps to navigate the word maze!

  • 1. Goal: Connect your start word (e.g., CAT) to the target word (e.g., BAGEL) in as few tries as possible.
  • 2. Pick Your Path: Each turn gives you 3 words. One option leads closer, while others branch into detours or dead ends.
  • 3. Follow the Logic: Words link by natural associations! (e.g., CAT → FISH → OCEAN → SALT → FLOUR → BAKERY → BAGEL).
  • 4. Keep Exploring: Hit a dead end? Don't worry—just keep picking new words until you steer yourself back on track!
  • 5. Rank Up: Solve the puzzle in fewer steps to climb to the top of the leaderboard.
